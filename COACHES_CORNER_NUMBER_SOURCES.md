## Summary

- 38 rows (tennis 18, golf 20). Repeated claims across files are grouped into one row.
- Verdicts: **CONFIRMED 6** (including 2 partial: tour proximity 23-30 ft, and the OB rule row, whose ~2.0 half is unsourced). **WRONG 1**. **UNSOURCED-2nd 9** (a secondary source supports the value). **UNSOURCED 22**.
- **WRONG: servePlusOne.ts:63 and :99.** 58% / 23% are ATP Infosys "Serve Effectiveness" (aces + unreturned + attacking first ball), not "attackable +1 ball". The +1 attack portion of the 58% is only 20%.
- Fixes worth making though not strictly WRONG:
  - serveTarget.ts:72: top servers' first-serve points are probably ~77-81%, not 70-75%.
  - passOrLob.ts:152 / :181: the 72% lob-to-smash figure is for all lobs; the "mid-court" qualifier is invented.
  - goOrLay 2.92 @100 yds and suckerPin's 5% three-putt at 20 ft: both read as amateur, not tour, values. The tour baseline is 2.80 and ~2.2%. Say whose baseline it is.
  - thePinch 46/48%: this is the 20-handicap Shot Scope figure.
  - servePlusOne 22%/18%: this is the down-the-line **backhand** figure.
  - approachOrStay 73%/21%: from a grass-court study.
- Most tennis numbers trace to Tennis Abstract (secondary). Most golf E-values are Broadie-style model numbers with no primary page; the PGA Tour baseline table itself is published only in Broadie's book and paper.
- Limits: pgatour.com stat pages, MyGolfSpy and the ATP leaderboard could not be opened (403 or JavaScript-rendered). I couldn't extract text from the Broadie PDF.
